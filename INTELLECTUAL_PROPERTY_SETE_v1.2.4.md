## 3. SMDG-Clustering

SMDG-Clustering is proposed as a research construct in which goals themselves can become objects of structured clustering, comparison and adaptive reassessment.

For this version the primary goal cluster is:

**Local Life Quality + Clean and Sustainable Nature**

## 4. Practical Multi-Layer Blind Spots

PMLBS is proposed as a tourism-system construct for identifying situations in which one analytical layer appears successful while another layer is deteriorating or unobserved.

The provisional layers are:

1. Individual / Household
2. Community / Social
3. Economic / Infrastructure
4. Environmental / Ecological
5. Governance / System

## 5. HMC-GATE

HMC-GATE is a proposed second-order human metacognitive governance layer. It reviews not only outputs, but also objectives, assumptions, indicators, constraints and consequences.
