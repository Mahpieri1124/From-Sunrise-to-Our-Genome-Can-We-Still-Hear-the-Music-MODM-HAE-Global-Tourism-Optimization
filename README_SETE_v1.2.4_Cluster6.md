## 1. Purpose of Cluster (-6)

This layer deliberately narrows the enormous SETE vision to two immediate, measurable and human-readable outcome families:

1. **Local Life Quality**
2. **Clean and Sustainable Nature**

The working proposition is:

> **A tourism intervention should preferably create local human value and environmental value simultaneously, rather than improving one by systematically degrading the other.**

This is a research proposition, not an empirical guarantee.

## 2. One Shot — Two Goal

The cluster is intentionally designed as:

```text
                 SETE INTERVENTION
                       |
             +---------+---------+
             |                   |
             v                   v
      LOCAL LIFE QUALITY   CLEAN + SUSTAINABLE
                                NATURE
             |                   |
             +---------+---------+
                       |
                JOINT OUTCOME
                       |
                 MEASURE → LEARN
                       |
                     ADAPT
                       ↺
```

The system must also test when the two goals conflict.

## 3. SMDG-Clustering

**Smart Multi Dimensional Goal Clustering** extends the earlier SETE clustering logic.

Earlier clustering focused on tourists, preferences and destinations. This layer adds:

> **Goals themselves as structured objects of clustering, comparison and adaptive reassessment.**

For Cluster (-6):

**G = {Local Life Quality, Clean & Sustainable Nature}**

The construct is proposed and pre-empirical.

## 4. Practical Multi-Layer Blind Spots

A system can appear successful at one analytical layer while important consequences remain invisible at another.

Examples:

- tourist arrivals ↑ while resident wellbeing ↓;
- local revenue ↑ while biodiversity ↓;
- employment ↑ while housing pressure ↑;
- ecological indicators ↑ while local livelihoods ↓.

PMLBS therefore asks:

> **What is improving at one layer while another layer is deteriorating?**

### Provisional layers

1. Individual / Household
2. Community / Social
3. Economic / Infrastructure
4. Environmental / Ecological
5. Governance / System

These are proposed analytical categories, not fixed universal laws.

## 5. HMC-GATE Role

HMC-GATE asks not only:

> “Did the intervention achieve its target?”

but:

> “What did the target fail to measure?”

and:

> “Which layer may be deteriorating while another appears successful?”

Thus HMC-GATE becomes a second-order review of objectives, assumptions, indicators, trade-offs and consequences.

## 6. Evolution Before Perfection

The implementation principle is:

**Start → Observe → Detect Blind Spots → Triage Challenges → Improve → Measure → Learn → Adapt**

This continues the earlier **Metacognitive Challenge Triage (MCT)** and **Technical Challenge Non-Dominance (TCND)** direction.

“No dominant technical challenge” does **not** mean that technical problems are ignored. It means they should not automatically terminate the underlying humanitarian/environmental objective.

They should be:

**classified → decomposed → prioritised → tested → mitigated → redesigned → progressively solved.**

## 7. Relation to Previous SETE Architecture

Earlier SETE:

**Global Tourist Diversity → Preference Spectrum → Adaptive Clustering → Destination Capability Mapping → Tourist–Destination–Time Matching → Capacity Balancing → Multi-Objective Optimisation → Adaptive Feedback → AI-Assisted Decision Intelligence → Human Metacognitive Governance → HMC-GATE**

Cluster (-6) adds:

**SMDG-Clustering → Practical Multi-Layer Blind-Spot Detection → Local Life Quality + Clean Sustainable Nature → HMC-GATE Reassessment**

This is an evolutionary extension, not a replacement.

## 8. Scientific Position

The literature already establishes that tourism can affect local quality of life positively and negatively; smart tourism ecosystems exist; tourism has environmental impacts; community-based tourism addresses empowerment and sustainability; and multi-objective optimisation has been applied to tourism carrying capacity.

Therefore this version does **not** claim that its two goals are individually unprecedented.

The proposed contribution is the **specific operational integration** of:

**SMDG-Clustering + Practical Multi-Layer Blind Spots + One-Shot-Two-Goal + HMC-GATE + Evolution Before Perfection**

within SETE.

## 9. Core Research Questions

1. Can tourism interventions improve local quality of life while protecting nature?
2. Can the two outcomes be represented as a primary goal cluster?
3. Can SMDG-Clustering reveal goal conflicts and synergies?
4. Can PMLBS detect cross-layer consequences hidden by conventional optimisation?
5. Can HMC-GATE improve detection of consequential blind spots?
6. Can disadvantaged destinations gain local value without unacceptable ecological pressure?
7. Can the architecture work with incomplete data and improve progressively?
8. Does the architecture outperform simpler tourism-management baselines?
9. Under what conditions does One-Shot-Two-Goal fail?

## 10. Preliminary Success Criterion

A SETE intervention is promising only when measurable improvement in local life-quality indicators is accompanied by non-degrading or improving environmental indicators, subject to destination-specific constraints and uncertainty.

This is a hypothesis for testing.
