## Methodological Note

The references above are not presented as proof of SETE. They establish the surrounding research landscape and identify established components against which the proposed architecture can be compared.

The central novelty claim should remain limited to the proposed synthesis, architectural integration, metacognitive challenge-management layer and progressive validation logic.
