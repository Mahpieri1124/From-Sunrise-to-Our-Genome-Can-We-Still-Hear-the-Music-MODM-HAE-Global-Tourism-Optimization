## Description

Version 1.2.4 introduces Layer (j), Cluster (-6) within the Smart Evolutionary Tourism Ecosystem (SETE).

The layer deliberately focuses on two immediate outcome families:

1. **Local Life Quality**
2. **Clean and Sustainable Nature**

The central proposition is not that tourism automatically creates these outcomes. The proposition is that SETE can investigate whether tourism interventions can be designed and adaptively managed so that local human wellbeing and environmental sustainability become **simultaneously visible, measurable and jointly governable**.

### Practical Multi-Layer Blind Spots

The version introduces **Practical Multi-Layer Blind Spots (PMLBS)**.

A blind spot exists when a system appears successful at one analytical level while an important consequence is deteriorating at another.

Examples include:

- economic benefit ↑ / biodiversity ↓;
- tourism employment ↑ / housing pressure ↑;
- local revenue ↑ / resident wellbeing ↓;
- environmental improvement ↑ / local livelihood ↓.

The provisional layers are:

1. Individual / Household
2. Community / Social
3. Economic / Infrastructure
4. Environmental / Ecological
5. Governance / System

### Smart Multi Dimensional Goal Clustering

**SMDG-Clustering** is proposed as a goal-centric extension of the earlier SETE clustering architecture.

For Cluster (-6):

**G = {Local Life Quality, Clean & Sustainable Nature}**

The research then asks whether the two goals reinforce each other, conflict, or shift costs across layers.

### Evolution Before Perfection

The implementation loop is:

**Start → Observe → Detect Blind Spots → Triage Challenges → Improve → Measure → Learn → Adapt**

This continues the earlier Metacognitive Challenge Triage (MCT) and Technical Challenge Non-Dominance (TCND) principles.

Technical challenges remain real and must be handled rigorously. They should not, however, automatically become the final authority over the underlying humanitarian/environmental objective.

### Relationship to the broader SETE architecture

**Global Tourist Diversity → Preference Spectrum → Adaptive Clustering → Destination Capability Mapping → Tourist–Destination–Time Matching → Capacity Balancing → Multi-Objective Optimisation → Adaptive Feedback → AI-Assisted Decision Intelligence → Human Metacognitive Governance → HMC-GATE**

Cluster (-6) adds:

**SMDG-Clustering → Practical Multi-Layer Blind-Spot Detection → Local Life Quality + Clean Sustainable Nature → HMC-GATE Reassessment**

### Research status

The complete architecture is conceptual, hypothesis-generating and pre-empirical.

The two goals are strongly grounded in existing tourism literature; the proposed contribution lies in their **specific goal-clustering, blind-spot and metacognitive integration within SETE**.
